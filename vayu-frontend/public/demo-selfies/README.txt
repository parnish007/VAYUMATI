DEMO SELFIE IMAGES — VayuMitti Community Wall
=============================================

Drop your masked selfie photos here. The wall will pick them up automatically.

REQUIRED FILENAMES (exactly as shown):
  p1.jpg   — person 1 with mask
  p2.jpg   — person 2 with mask
  p3.jpg   — person 3 with mask
  p4.jpg   — person 4 (no mask, shows "No mask" overlay as contrast)
  p5.jpg   — person 5 with mask
  p6.jpg   — person 6 with mask
  p7.jpg   — person 7 with mask
  p8.jpg   — person 8 with mask
  p9.jpg   — person 9 (no mask, shows "No mask" overlay as contrast)

SUPPORTED FORMATS: .jpg / .jpeg / .png / .webp
RECOMMENDED SIZE: 400×533px (3:4 portrait ratio — matches card layout)

If any file is missing, the card falls back to a gradient avatar with initials.
No restart needed — Next.js serves from /public/ directly.

FULL PATH FROM REPO ROOT:
  vayu-frontend/public/demo-selfies/p1.jpg  (etc.)

URL IN BROWSER:
  http://localhost:3000/demo-selfies/p1.jpg
