MOBILE / WARD MEMBER — HOME SCREEN
Greeting uses the user's name and the time of day. The AQI hero card dominates — the number must be legible from arm's length. People Counter makes the abstract tangible. Advisory strip is always visible, never buried in a tab. No hamburger menus on mobile — everything reachable in two taps via bottom nav.

① AQI Hero Card
Legible from 1 meter. Color = severity. Gauge gives visual weight. PM2.5 is always the sub-number — it's the WHO reference metric.
② Risk Row
Two numbers the user owns personally: their body's exposure and their field's health. Not aggregate data — personal stakes.
③ Advisory Strip
Always on home screen. Never in a sub-page. Action chips are the actual behavioral outputs — tap to log the action and earn PA score points.
④ People Counter
47,000 is harder to ignore than "AQI 167". The counter makes pollution collective, not just personal. Updates every 30 seconds via SSE.
⑤ Bottom Nav
5 tabs. No hamburger. Active tab is the only green element in the nav — draws the eye immediately. Labels always visible.
MOBILE / WARD MEMBER — PERSONAL EXPOSURE TRACKER
This is the most interaction-heavy mobile screen. Key principle: adding a stop must feel like sending a WhatsApp message, not filling a government form. Timeline is horizontal scroll — no vertical chart that competes with the route list. The WHO bar is always visible as context.

Dose Number
The μg number is the raw data. The cigarettes are the meaning. Show both but make the cigarettes the emotional anchor — it triggers visceral understanding immediately.
WHO Bar
Always at the top of the screen. The gradient (green→amber→red) communicates severity progression without a legend. "3 days left" makes the weekly budget feel finite and real.
Horizontal Timeline
Scrolls left-to-right chronologically. Current hour is highlighted with a green border. Bar height = AQI. Color = severity category. Tap a bar for tooltip. Never a static chart.
Route as Bubbles
Each stop is a chat bubble, not a form row. Add Stop button looks like a WhatsApp "+" — the mental model is familiar. AQI is auto-populated from nearest sensor — user just sets the place and time.
MOBILE / WARD MEMBER — REWARDS & BADGES
The PA score ring is the identity anchor of this screen. Users should feel ownership over their score the way they feel ownership over a step count. Badges mix earned (full color, glowing) and locked (greyed, with one-sentence unlock condition on tap). The reward card shows what is achievable — even if the physical prize is still a few weeks away.

PA Score Ring
Gradient green→amber matches the reward tension: you're doing well but there's still room. The rank below the ring is the social hook — people are fundamentally competitive. "You are #7" is more motivating than "Score: 68".
Reward Card
The progress bar must never be full. If the user reaches top 3, replace with next tier. The reward must always feel four points away — achievable but not trivial.
Badge States
Earned badges glow. Locked badges show the icon (so the user knows what's coming) but greyed. Tapping a locked badge shows the exact unlock condition — removes ambiguity and gives a micro-goal.
MATI Tip
The agent personalises one specific action the user can take this week to advance. Not generic advice — a specific day, route, and outcome. This is generated by the compute_protective_score tool.

Share Button Visibility
The "Share Your Mask" button only appears when ward AQI > 150. It's invisible on clean days. This makes it contextually meaningful — not spam. On demo day with AQI 167, it's visible and prominent.
The Green Glow
The glow on selfie thumbnails is not an animation — it's a steady warm green box-shadow. It only appears when compliance crosses 30%. It's earned, not default. Judges notice the absence of glow on a low-compliance ward immediately.
Ward Board Anchoring
User's ward is always pinned visible with a ★ marker and amber highlight — even if it's #11 of 12. They can always see where they stand. The "improved 2 positions" note is the hook that makes them want to come back next week.
Difficulty Multiplier
Ward 11 is near kilns. Without the multiplier, it would always rank last — making the board unfair and demoralizing. The multiplier rewards the community for what they can control (PA actions) not what they can't (industrial proximity).
DESKTOP / WARD EXECUTIVE — SENSOR NODE GRID
The ward executive's primary job is network health, not personal exposure. This view shows all nodes with their live status, battery, signal, and latest readings. An offline banner fires immediately when any node drops. Three deployment option visual is shown above the grid.
